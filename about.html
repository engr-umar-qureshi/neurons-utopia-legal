About the channel

One fascinating thing, every single day.

Neurons Utopia is a daily short-video channel for people who love learning something new before breakfast. Four series, one minute max, zero fluff.

📜 History mysteries

Caligula's floating palaces, Rome's hundred days of games, the fridge inventor's strange grave. Stories historians still argue about — told straight, with the receipts.

🔬 Science, simply

Why the Moon shows one face, how many smells you really smell, the berries hiding in your fruit bowl. Big ideas in plain words.

✍️ Quotes that stick

Marcus Aurelius by candlelight and other lines worth keeping — set to music, made to replay.

✏️ Doodles & shapes

Whiteboard sketches drawn live, stroke by stroke, plus neon shape-toons for the youngest curious minds.

Our promise

Under a minute, every day. If a video wastes your time, we failed — tell us.

Family-friendly
