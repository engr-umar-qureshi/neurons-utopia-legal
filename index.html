How publishing works

This site is the home of the channel. Finished videos are posted to our social accounts. Posting to TikTok uses the official Content Posting API: it uploads videos we created to our own connected TikTok account with captions we write, each video published only with the creator's explicit approval. We never post on anyone else's behalf and never read anyone else's data. Details: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.
